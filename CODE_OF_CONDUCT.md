# Community standards

Awake Launcher welcomes people of every background and experience level. Treat other participants with respect, including when you disagree or give feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual advances, personal attacks, and sharing someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. These standards apply to project discussions, contributions, and people representing the project.

Maintainers may remove content or limit participation when these standards are violated. Report concerns to the [Awake Launcher maintainers](https://github.com/awakenginexe).

This short policy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
